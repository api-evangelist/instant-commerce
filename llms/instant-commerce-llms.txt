# Instant Commerce

> Instant Commerce (product: Instant, instant.so) is an AI-native eCommerce software company building an AI-powered, no-code page builder for Shopify. Merchants describe what they want and Instant's AI agents generate complete store pages — layouts, copy, and imagery — with Figma-to-Shopify conversion, A/B testing, and conversion-rate optimization. It serves ~25,000 brands, ships an official SDK for custom React blocks, and runs a hosted MCP server. Backed by HV Capital (~$10M raised). Based in Amsterdam and Miami.
